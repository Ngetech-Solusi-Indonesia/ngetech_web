## Platform

web

## Users
Three audiences with equal weight, all in or near Bandung:
- Business owners and small-to-medium businesses (UMKM) whose operations still run on paper or spreadsheets (stock, attendance, records).
- Schools and institutions (principals, admin offices, foundations) that need attendance and internal systems.
- Event organisers, wedding vendors, and brand activations that need a photobooth.

Their job on this site: understand what Ngetech can build for them and start a conversation on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
Ngetech Solusi Indonesia is a five-person software team in Bandung that builds custom software, including software that runs with physical devices. Success is a visitor opening a WhatsApp chat about a project.

## Positioning
Confirmed differentiators (user-confirmed 2026-10-07):
- One team handles both software and hardware (RFID readers, cameras, printers).
- Local to Bandung; the team can come on site to install and train.
- Built to the client's own workflow, not an off-the-shelf subscription app.
- Scale and cost that make sense for UMKM.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Products and their true status (capabilities confirmed from the org repos, 2026-10-07):
- **Inventory system** (repo Inventory-Management-Final): purchases from suppliers, cash/credit sales, warehouse stock, customer receivables with instalments, profit dashboard. In use by a real client (client not named).
- **NgeBooth** (repo Ngebooth, latest work on branch feat/booth-editor-ux): web photobooth platform with strip templates, photo editor, QR code + passcode sharing, vendor dashboard (pricing, vouchers, booth licences), camera and printer bridge. Internal testing.
- **RFID attendance** (repo School-Project): school attendance with ESP32 RFID readers, students/teachers/classes/schedules, reports. In development, not deployed anywhere.
